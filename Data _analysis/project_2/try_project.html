Step 1: Ask (Business Understanding)

Define the problem to guide analysis.

  • What are the total revenue (from costs) and average trip duration?
  • Which bike type (e.g., Electric vs. City) generates more revenue or longer trips?
  • How do trip categories (Casual, Commute, etc.) compare in volume and revenue?
  • Are there seasonal trends (e.g., summer peaks)?
  • What recommendations for optimizing bike fleet or promotions?

Assumptions: No cancellations; focus on 2023; revenue based on cost model. Success Metric: Insights for 10-15% usage increase (e.g., target low-season promotions).

Output: Note your questions in Excel/Word.

Time Estimate: 10-15 minutes.

Step 2: Process (Data Preparation)

Clean and prepare in Excel.

  1. Import Data: Paste the table or use the downloaded CSV into Sheet1 (A1:E51). Format: A:A as Date; D:E as Number.
  2. Calculate New Columns:
    • "Total Cost" (F1): `=D2*E2/10` in F2, drag down.
    • "Month" (G1): `=TEXT(A2,"MMMM")` in G2, drag down.
  3. Clean Data:
    • Remove duplicates: Data > Remove Duplicates (A:G).
    • Fix issues: No blanks here; if any, fill with averages (`=AVERAGE(D:D)`).
    • Validate: Durations >0; no negatives.
  4. Save: As "BikeTrips_Cleaned.xlsx".

Output: Sheet with 50 rows, new columns. Total revenue: ~$300-400 (scaled small for beginners).

Time Estimate: 15-20 minutes.

Step 3: Analyze (Exploratory Data Analysis)

Compute insights in Excel.

  1. Basic Stats ("Analysis" Sheet):
    • Total Revenue: `=SUM('Sheet1'!F:F)`.
    • Avg Duration: `=AVERAGE('Sheet1'!D:D)`.
    • Trips: `=COUNTA('Sheet1'!A2:A51)` → 50.
  2. Bike Type Breakdown (PivotTable):
    • Insert > PivotTable > A:G. Rows: Bike Type; Values: Sum Total Cost, Avg Duration.
    • Insights: Electric Bikes may have higher revenue due to longer/costlier trips.
  3. Trip Category Analysis:
    • Pivot: Rows: Trip Category; Values: Count of Date (volume), Sum Total Cost.
    • Insights: Commute might dominate volume; Tourist higher cost.
  4. Seasonal Trends:
    • Pivot: Rows: Month; Values: Sum Total Cost, Count of Date.
    • Insights: Peaks in summer (Jul-Aug); Dips in winter (Nov-Dec).
  5. Advanced:
    • Cost per Minute: New column `=E2/D2`. Pivot by Bike Type → Identify efficient types.

Insights: Electric bikes drive revenue; Promote in low months for growth.

Time Estimate: 20-30 minutes.

Step 4: Visualize (Create Dashboards)

Use Tableau for visuals.

  1. Setup: Connect to "BikeTrips_Cleaned.xlsx" > Sheet1.
  2. Build Charts:
    • Bar: Revenue by Bike Type: Columns: SUM(Total Cost); Rows: Bike Type. Sort descending.
    • Line: Monthly Trends: Columns: Month (from Date); Rows: SUM(Total Cost). Line type.
    • Bar: Trips by Category: Columns: COUNT(Date); Rows: Trip Category.
  3. Dashboard:
    • New Dashboard > Drag charts. Add Filter: Bike Type (global).
    • Text: "Insight: Summer peaks account for 30% of revenue."
  4. Publish: Save to Tableau Public; share link.

Output: Interactive dashboard for portfolio.

Time Estimate: 20-30 minutes.
