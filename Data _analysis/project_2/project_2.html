Bike Trip Trends Analysis: 2023 Dataset

Project Overview
Analyzed 50 bike trip records from a fictional city bike-sharing service using Excel and Chart.js for a responsive, interactive dashboard (inspired by Tableau dashboard.twb). Total revenue: $326.50. Average duration: 25.7 min. Presented: September 15, 2025, 01:01 PM IST.

Questions Asked & Answers

Q1: What are the total revenue and average trip duration?
A: Total Revenue: $326.50; Average Duration: 25.7 minutes.
Q2: Which bike type generates the most revenue or longest trips?
A: Road Bike leads revenue ($112.00); Mountain Bike has longest avg. duration (26.5 min).
Q3: How do trip categories compare in volume and revenue?
A: Commute: 15 trips (highest volume, $88.40 revenue); Tourist: $89.70 revenue.
Q4: Are there seasonal trends?
A: Peaks in July ($57.30); Dips in June ($4.00) and January ($14.60).
Q5: What recommendations for optimization?
A: Increase Road/Electric Bike fleet in summer; Promote off-peak (winter) for 10-15% growth.

Step-by-Step Process
1. Ask: Defined 5 key questions on revenue, trends, and optimization (see above).
2. Process: Imported to Excel; calculated Total Cost (=Duration*Cost/10); added Month; cleaned data.
3. Analyze: PivotTables identified Road Bike revenue lead; July peak.
4. Visualize: Built responsive dashboard with Chart.js (inspired by dashboard.twb); added filters and animations.

Key Insights

  • Road Bikes contribute 34% of revenue—prioritize fleet expansion.
  • July-August summer peaks (30% of revenue)—target seasonal campaigns.
  • Commute trips (30% volume) have lower margins; focus on Tourist/Sport upsell.
  • Growth potential: 10-15% with off-peak incentives.
Recommendations: Increase Road/Electric Bike stock in peak months; Launch winter promotions.
