Movie Ratings Analysis: 2023 Dataset

Project Overview:
This project analyzes user ratings for 10 movies from 15 users. We explored patterns in preferences, activity levels, genre popularity, time trends, and country-specific behavior to uncover actionable insights.

Detailed Insights

  • Top Movies: Interstellar (4.8), Inception (4.7), and The Dark Knight (4.6) consistently ranked highest, showing strong audience preference for Sci-Fi and action-driven narratives.
  • Most Active Users: Alice (12 ratings), Bob (9), and Charlie (8) contributed most ratings, highlighting core engaged users who drive the platform's activity.
  • Genre Trends: Sci-Fi led with an average rating of 4.6, followed by Drama (4.4). Animation and Comedy trailed behind, suggesting opportunities to improve those genres' appeal.
  • Time Trends: Average ratings rose steadily from January (4.2) to April (4.6), showing that newer releases gained stronger positive reception over time.
  • Country Patterns: USA and India were the most active regions, accounting for more than 60% of ratings, making them priority markets for growth strategies.
