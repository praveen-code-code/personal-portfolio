Phase 1: Ask (Define Questions and Objectives)

Clarify the business problem and define key questions:

  • What are the total sales and average sales per transaction?
  • Which category (Electronics vs. Clothing) generates more revenue?
  • What are the top-selling products?
  • Are there monthly trends or seasonal patterns in sales?
  • How does quantity sold compare to revenue across categories?

Objective: Recommend focus areas (e.g., marketing strategies). Document these in Excel notes.

Time Estimate: 10-15 minutes.

Phase 2: Cleaning (Prepare Data in Excel)

Steps to prepare the data:

  1. Import Data: Paste the table or use the downloaded CSV into Excel Sheet1 (A1 start).
  2. Format Columns:
    • "Date": Format as Date (MM/DD/YYYY).
    • "Quantity Sold" and "Unit Price": Format as Number.
  3. Check and Fix Issues:
    • Duplicates: Use Data tab > Remove Duplicates (none here).
    • Missing Values: None here, but use =AVERAGE(range) if needed.
  4. Add Derived Columns:
    • "Total Sales" (F1): In F2, formula `=D2*E2`. Drag down.
    • "Month" (G1): In G2, `=TEXT(A2, "MMMM")`. Drag down.
  5. Validate: Spot-check (e.g., 2 * 800 = 1600). Save as "SalesData_Cleaned.xlsx". Note: For missing dates, use today's date (09/17/2025) if needed.
Time Estimate: 15-20 minutes.

Phase 3: Analyze (Explore Insights in Excel)

Use Excel to extract insights:

  1. Basic Metrics:
    • Total Sales: `=SUM(F2:F21)` (~$13,895).
    • Average Sales: `=AVERAGE(F2:F21)` (~$694.75).
    • Category Breakdown: PivotTable (Insert > PivotTable on A1:G21) with "Category" in Rows, "Total Sales" in Values (Sum). Result: Electronics ~$10,450, Clothing ~$3,445.
  2. Deeper Exploration:
    • Top Products: PivotTable with "Product" in Rows, "Total Sales" in Values > Sort descending (e.g., Smartphone $2,500).
    • Monthly Trends: PivotTable with "Month" in Rows, "Total Sales" in Values (e.g., February peak at $2,900).
    • Quantity vs. Revenue: PivotTable with "Category" in Rows, "Quantity Sold" and "Total Sales" in Values (Clothing: 120 units, $3,445; Electronics: 55 units, $10,450).
  3. Simple Charts: From PivotTables, Insert > Bar/Line Charts (e.g., sales by month).
  4. Document Findings: Note insights in a summary sheet.
Time Estimate: 20-30 minutes.

Phase 4: Share (Visualize and Report in Tableau)

Steps to visualize in Tableau Public:

  1. Connect Data: Open Tableau Public > Connect > Excel > "SalesData_Cleaned.xlsx" > Drag Sheet1.
  2. Build Visuals:
    • Sales by Category: "Category" to Rows, "Total Sales" (Sum) to Columns > Bar Chart > Sort.
    • Monthly Trends: "Month" to Columns, "Total Sales" to Rows > Line Chart.
    • Top Products: "Product" to Rows, "Total Sales" to Columns > Bar Chart > Sort descending.
  3. Create Dashboard: Dashboard > New > Drag sheets in. Add filters (e.g., Category) and titles.
  4. Share Outputs: Export as PDF/image or share via Tableau Public link.
Time Estimate: 20-30 minutes.

Phase 5: Conclusion (Summarize and Recommend)

Key Takeaways:

  • Total sales: $13,895, with Electronics dominating (~75% or $10,450).
  • Trends: Peaks in February ($2,900) and March ($2,220); dips in November ($200).
  • Clothing: Higher volume (120 units) but lower revenue ($3,445).

Recommendations: Prioritize Electronics marketing; bundle Clothing for volume growth. Monitor holiday impacts.

Time Estimate: 10 minutes. Total Project Time: ~1.5-2 hours.
